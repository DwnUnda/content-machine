# Content Rules

## Core Purpose
Home Dry Lab content should help Australian homeowners and renters make better decisions about drying, damp, mould, humidity, laundry, indoor air quality, and related products.

## Language
- Use Australian English.
- Prefer `mould`, `colour`, `favourite`, `power bill`, `laundry`, and `running cost`.
- Avoid American replacements unless quoting a source directly.

## Tone
- Plain English
- Practical
- Direct
- Helpful
- Natural
- Research-backed
- Human-sounding
- Not corporate
- Not overly polished
- Not fake-expert sounding

## Forbidden AI Slop
- In today's fast-paced world
- When it comes to
- Dive into
- Unlock
- Game changer
- Elevate
- It is important to note
- Whether you're
- Robust solution
- Seamless
- Revolutionise
- Cutting-edge
- Designed to meet your needs
- Perfect for every home

## Evidence Rules
- Never invent prices, specs, warranties, availability, ratings, review counts, test results, health claims, or personal experience.
- If data is missing, say `Not confirmed` or leave the claim out.
- Never claim hands-on testing unless `personally_tested=true`.
- If a product was not personally tested, use honest sourcing language.

## Product Recommendation Standard
Every recommended product section must include:
- Best for
- Why it made the list
- Key specs
- What buyers seem to like
- Common complaints
- Who should buy it
- Who should avoid it
- Bottom line

## Original Value Requirement
Every article must add original value beyond competitors. Include at least three of:
- real user complaint summary
- buyer review pattern analysis
- Australian climate context
- product suitability matrix
- who should avoid this guidance
- room size or use-case decision guide
- running cost discussion
- rental-friendly advice
- common mistakes section
- internal link recommendations

## Health And Damp Claims
- Use careful wording.
- Do not make medical claims.
- Prefer Australian government, health, or other authoritative sources.
- Do not overstate what a dehumidifier or air purifier can do.
- Explain that moisture control can reduce conditions that support mould, but does not magically fix severe mould problems.
