# Article Quality Standard

## Money Articles Must Include
- quick answer
- comparison table
- how we researched
- product recommendations
- buyer decision guide
- common mistakes
- FAQ
- final recommendation

## Informational Articles Must Include
- direct answer near the top
- clear explanation
- Australian home context
- practical steps
- when to consider a product
- when to seek professional help if relevant
- FAQ

## QA Scorecard
Score every draft against:
- Australian English
- no fake testing claims
- no unsupported specs
- no AI slop phrases
- clear search intent match
- clear recommendations
- product drawbacks included
- Australian context included
- evidence-backed claims
- internal links suggested
- readable formatting
- original value included

## Ready For Review Gate
- Score below 85: not ready for review by default
- Score 85 or above: eligible for ready-for-review
- Manual override can bypass the gate, but must be explicit and logged

## Final Publishing Standard
The draft should feel like a strong Home Dry Lab draft awaiting final polish, not a generic AI article or thin affiliate page.
